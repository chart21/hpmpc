\subsection{Weights known to none (UC1) and to all (UC3)}\label{sec:kpw}

Besides UC2, the default, two use cases change who holds the weights. In UC1 (\flag{A_KNOWN=0}) both parties hold
shares of the weight masks as well as of the activation masks, so the conv, FC and BN triples are AB triples for
which both parties encrypt. In UC3 (public weights, \flag{PUBLIC_WEIGHTS=1}) the linear layers multiply locally
and need no triples at all. We run public weights with \flag{TRUNC_DELAYED=1} and
\flag{BIT_INJECTION_TRUNC_SIM=1}: each linear layer's truncation is deferred and folded into the next
ReLU's bit injection instead of costing a round of its own. \Cref{fig:settings} compares the three use cases for
the plain family.

\paragraph{UC3: public weights.} Only the ReLU material remains to be preprocessed: across all 30 variants,
preprocessing takes 0.92--2.03\s per 10 images and 3.5--14.3\s per 192 images, 16--41\% and 22--61\%
less than the same variant in UC2 in the final builds. The online phase takes 0.27--0.41\s and 0.33--0.42\s
(9--33\% and 30--50\% less): convolutions send nothing, and the truncation shares the bit injection's message. With the AdamW model, the UC3 variants
classify 63--73 of 100 images in single batch and 115--131 of 192 (60--68\%) in multi-batch. Unfused
BatchNorm needed the first-layer truncation fix of \cref{sec:correct}; before it, those variants classified 0 of 10 images.

\paragraph{UC1: weights known to none.} With \flag{A_KNOWN=0} the linear-layer triples are AB triples, and the preprocessing traffic
grows by a factor of 1.4--1.9 (RCA, unfused BN, 10 images: 229 to 428\,MB). In single batch, preprocessing takes 1.34--2.74\s over the 30 variants and the online phase 0.34--0.52\s. Since the
concurrent evaluation (\cref{sec:he}), \flag{A_KNOWN=1} is faster in both HE phases of every variant (conv 0.27--0.33 against
0.30--0.39\s, BN 0.20--0.24 against 0.23--0.26\s); the totals differ by $-0.07$ to $+0.13\s$, within the noise of the
OT phases. In multi-batch, with the lane batching (\cref{sec:multi}), UC1 takes 8.3--20.6\s over the 30 variants
(before it, 13.8--26.4\s), 0.4--1.3\s (2--14\%) more than UC2; the online phase takes 0.56--0.78\s. With the
AdamW model UC1 classifies 64--72 of 100 images in single batch and 116--133 of 192 in multi-batch. Its accuracy first looked like
chance level: a bisection over the added optimizations found the threaded GEMM (with the extra threads off, 5 of 10 correct)
and, for unfused BN, the BatchNorm triples, both described in \cref{sec:correct}.
